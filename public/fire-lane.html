Fire Lane - Emergency Access Only

While a dedicated fire lane from 1965 ensures emergency vehicle access, it creates new challenges. Without bike lanes, cyclists and cars must share the same lane, forcing cars to travel at bicycle speeds since passing in the fire lane is prohibited.

According to California Vehicle Code 22500.1, no vehicle may stop, park, or stand in a designated fire lane except when necessary to avoid traffic conflicts or when directed by law enforcement.

Modern 2025 bike lanes offer a better solution: they separate bicycle and vehicle traffic, allowing each to move at their natural speeds. When emergency vehicles approach, the bike lanes provide ample space for cars to pull over safely, maintaining efficient emergency access while improving daily traffic flow for everyone.
